House rules

These rules keep The Old Mill pleasant and safe for everyone. They form part of your agreement, and we may update them from time to time with reasonable notice.

  1. Keys and securityLock your space, and close and lock the external doors and gates when you are last out. Don't copy keys or lend them out. Report lost keys straight away; replacements are charged at cost.
  2. Wi-FiWi-Fi details are in your tenant portal. Don't share them outside your business, and avoid very heavy use (such as large continuous downloads) that slows the connection for others.
  3. Parking and accessParking is on the street only; there is no on-site parking. Keep Mill Lane, the gates and the loading doors of other units clear at all times.
  4. DeliveriesArrange deliveries for when someone is on site. We can't accept or sign for parcels.
  5. WasteTake your waste away or use only the bins provided for your use. No trade waste in domestic bins.
  6. Shared areasKeep hallways, stairs and fire exits clear. Don't store anything in shared areas.
  7. Noise and smellsKeep noise, music and smells to a level that doesn't disturb other occupiers or neighbours.
  8. Smoking and vapingNot allowed anywhere inside the buildings.
  9. Fire safetyKnow your nearest fire exit. Don't prop open fire doors. No portable gas or paraffin heaters, candles or open flames.
  10. Heaters and appliancesTurn off heaters, lights and appliances when you leave. Heating is included on a fair-use basis. Ask before bringing in any high-power equipment. How to control your heaters.
  11. Hoist LoftThe storage floor below the Hoist Loft has a low ceiling (about 5 ft). Take care of your head and use it for storage only.
  12. Problems and repairsReport faults by email to pete@theoldmillkineton.co.uk. In an emergency call 07456 338728.
