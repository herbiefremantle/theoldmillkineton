Heaters: control them from your phone

Your room's electric heaters can be switched on and off from your phone, wherever you are, using the free Rointe Nexa app. Setup takes about 10 minutes.

Before you start

You need your phone, Bluetooth switched on, and The Old Mill Wi-Fi password. The Wi-Fi details are in your tenant portal.

Download the free Rointe Nexa app:

iPhone: App Store Android: Google Play Computer: rointenexa.com

Make sure you download Rointe Nexa, not the older "Rointe Connect" app, which is for older Rointe models.

The heaters connect to The Old Mill Wi-Fi, called TheOldMill_Network.

Set up your account

The app organises heating in three levels: an installation (the building), zones (rooms) inside it, and products (heaters) inside each zone.

  1. Open Rointe Nexa and create an account with your email address and a password. Confirm your email if the app asks.
  2. When asked to create an installation, name it The Old Mill and enter the address: Mill Lane, Kineton, Warwickshire, CV35 0LA.
  3. Allow the app to use Bluetooth and, if asked, location. Both are needed to find the heaters nearby.

Pair each heater

Do this in your room, standing next to the heater, with the heater switched on at the wall.

  1. On the heater itself, press Menu, go to Connectivity and choose Pair. Your phone can't see the heater until you do this.
  2. In the app, choose to add a product and follow the on-screen guide.
  3. When the app finds the heater, select it.
  4. When asked for Wi-Fi, choose TheOldMill_Network and enter its password from the tenant portal.
  5. Name the heater after your room and where it is, for example Hatch near wall, so you can tell your heaters apart.
  6. Repeat for each heater in your room.
Keep trying: pairing can take 4 or 5 attempts, but it will connect in the end.

The heater may update itself after pairing. Don't close the app or switch the heater off until it finishes. Rointe's own video shows the process: Rointe Nexa pairing tutorial.

Make a zone to control your heaters together

A zone groups the heaters in your room, so one tap switches them all on or off.

  1. In The Old Mill installation, create a new zone and name it after your room, for example Hatch.
  2. Put each of your heaters into that zone. If the app asked for a zone while pairing, choose the same zone for each one.
  3. Open the zone to switch all its heaters on or off, or set one temperature for them all.

Using it remotely: once paired, the heaters stay connected through The Old Mill Wi-Fi. You can switch the zone on before you arrive and off when you leave, from anywhere with mobile data or Wi-Fi.

The app can also run a weekly heating programme for the zone, so the room is warm when you usually arrive and switches off when you go home.

Fair use and help

Heating is included in your licence fee on a fair-use basis. Please switch your zone off when you leave, and don't leave heaters running overnight or when the room is empty.

If a heater won't pair or respond:

  • Check you chose TheOldMill_Network and typed the password correctly.
  • Put the heater back into pairing mode (Menu, Connectivity, Pair), restart your phone and try again, standing close to the heater.
  • If the internet or power goes off, the heaters keep their settings. Remote control comes back when the connection returns.
